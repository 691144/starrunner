The run

  • Sectors get faster and denser. Each one adds a new threat.
  • Glowing edges mark solid structure. Cyan frames are the way through.
  • Targeting computer locks the nearest turret or mine. Your bolts converge on it.
  • Close calls and kills build your multiplier, up to ×8. Taking a hit resets it.
  • Shield cells restore 30. Every sector gate restores 25.
